Lifecycle Protocol

From Himalayan Bedrock to Refined Ingot

Our closed-loop operational blueprint balances massive physical power with scientific environmental preservation at every elevation tier.

01

Exploration & LIDAR

Satellite multispectral analysis, terrain LIDAR scanning, and diamond core drilling down to 600m beneath alpine overburden.

02

Terrace Mining

Precision bench blasting utilizing digital delay detonators to eliminate seismic shockwaves on alpine slopes and minimize flyrock.

03

Zero-Cyanide Refining

Modern mechanical flotation and gravity separators without cyanide, coupled with closed-circuit 98% recycled industrial wash cycles.

04

Regional Logistics

Heavy telemetric multi-axle freight and high-tension ropeways transferring bulk materials directly to central national distribution hubs.
